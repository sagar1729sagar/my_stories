# Actor Sides: RAJU'S WIFE (30s)

**SAFETY** - An Original Short Film

## Emotional Arc

Raju's Wife is the quiet, tireless caregiver holding her shattered family together, rushing out to lift first her fallen daughter and then her husband. She takes in his tearful regret and the colleague's sobering account of the accident in silence, carrying her own grief without a word.

> Non-speaking role. Scene numbers match the shooting script. In other characters' dialogue, "..." means their lines are trimmed to the final sentence.

```
25  EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET - MORNING (FLASHBACK)

A school van stops at the gate of an independent house with a
small front lawn. The VAN DRIVER honks.

On a chair on the lawn sits RAJU (late 30s), wrapped in
bandages, plaster casts on one arm and one leg.

A 6-YEAR-OLD GIRL runs out. Halfway to the gate, she trips
and falls flat on her face. She bursts into tears.

Raju hauls himself up on his walking stick, takes one step
toward his daughter, loses his balance, and crashes to the
ground beside her.

RAJU'S WIFE (30s) rushes out of the house. She picks up the
girl first, then helps Raju to his feet. He rises slowly,
wincing.

The girl wipes her tears and walks to the van. Raju and his
wife watch her climb aboard.

                         RAJU
                    (to his wife)
          I couldn't even help my own child
          when she fell and cried right in
          front of me. All because I wasn't
          careful. I wish I could turn back
          time.

Tears roll down his cheeks.


29  INT. HOSPITAL - WARD - DAY (FLASHBACK WITHIN FLASHBACK)

Raju lies in a hospital bed, heavily bandaged. A COLLEAGUE
speaks quietly to Raju's Wife. The Girl stands beside her
mother.

                         COLLEAGUE
          ...If he had worn his PPE that day,
          the accident wouldn't have been this
          serious.
```
