                              SAFETY

                       An Original Short Film

                          SHOOTING SCRIPT






FADE IN:

1   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - PRE-DAWN

BLACK SCREEN. The house sleeps. Only the TICK of a wall clock.

EXTREME CLOSE UP - a cupboard handle. A hand eases it. The
door CREAKS open.

MEDIUM SHOT - KARTHIK (38) eases out his work clothes, as
quietly as he can. Morning shift today. He has to be out
before six.

LOW ANGLE, BED HEIGHT - In the bed, AADHYA (8) stirs. She
isn't alarmed. She knows this sound.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya. Her eyes half open. SHALLOW FOCUS: her
lashes sharp, Karthik a soft silhouette moving behind her.

AADHYA'S POV - The wall clock opposite her. It PULLS INTO
FOCUS: 5:20.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya closes her eyes and sinks back into sleep.

LATER - SAME ANGLE. Off screen, the front door CLICKS open.
Aadhya's eyes open lazily again.

INSERT - THE CLOCK: 5:45.

                         KARTHIK (O.S.)
          Bye, Sneha.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya bolts upright. FOCUS SNAPS to her face.

WIDE SHOT - She scrambles out of bed and hurries out of the
room, anxious. CAMERA WHIP PANS with her to the doorway.

                                                       CUT TO:

2   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

MEDIUM SHOT - Aadhya rounds the corner and stops short.

OVER THE SHOULDER (AADHYA) - Through the open front door,
Karthik stands outside with his back to her, strapping on
his motorcycle helmet. DEEP FOCUS: her shoulder soft in the
foreground, the helmet crisp in the doorway.

EXTREME CLOSE UP - The helmet strap CLICKS shut.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya. Her worried face softens into a small
smile. FOCUS HOLDS on her eyes.

WIDE SHOT - Through the doorway, Karthik rides off. SNEHA
(30s), Aadhya's mother, closes the door and turns.

MEDIUM TWO SHOT - She finds Aadhya standing there, smiling.

                         SNEHA
          You just missed Dad. He just left
          for office. Go back to sleep. I'll
          wake you up for school.

MEDIUM SHOT - Aadhya nods and pads back toward the bedroom,
still smiling. CAMERA HOLDS on Sneha watching her go.

                                                       CUT TO:

3   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - DAY

INSERT - THE WALL CLOCK: 3:30.

MEDIUM SHOT - Sneha sits on the sofa, waiting.

WIDE SHOT - The front door bursts open. Aadhya bounces in:
school uniform, bag on her back, lunch carrier swinging from
one hand.

MEDIUM SHOT - Sneha gets up to take the load off her.

TRACKING SHOT - Aadhya breezes right past her.

LOW ANGLE, FLOOR LEVEL - The bag THUMPS down. The lunch
carrier CLANKS beside it. Shoes, kicked off mid-stride,
tumble into frame.

TRACKING SHOT, FEET LEVEL - Socks hop-skip across the floor
to the sofa.

MEDIUM SHOT - She plops down and grabs Sneha's phone.

                         SNEHA
          Come on, Aadhya. You just came from
          outside. First take off those socks
          and dirty clothes and get fresh.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya doesn't hear a word.

INSERT - PHONE SCREEN. She opens WhatsApp and video calls
"DAD."

INSERT - PHONE SCREEN. Karthik appears in a safety helmet and
goggles. Behind him, the raw material yard of a steel plant.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya. Her eyes flick across the screen, a quick
searching glance, before she lights up.

                         AADHYA
          School's over for the day, Dad!

                         KARTHIK (ON PHONE)
          Nice. So how was school today?

                         AADHYA
          Good. It would've been amazing if
          there was no homework.

INSERT - PHONE SCREEN. Karthik laughs.

                         KARTHIK (ON PHONE)
          Okay, dear. Finish your homework
          quickly, before I get home. Then
          we can go out and play.

                         AADHYA
          Okay, Dad!

INSERT - Her thumb ends the call.

WIDE SHOT - Sneha has watched the whole thing from across the
room. RACK FOCUS from Aadhya on the sofa in the foreground to
Sneha in the background.

                         SNEHA
                    (to herself)
          Your mom is invisible to you.

MEDIUM SHOT - She heads into the kitchen. CAMERA HOLDS on the
empty kitchen doorway.

                                                       CUT TO:

4   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - EVENING

DING-DONG.

TRACKING SHOT - Aadhya races to the door and pulls it open.

MEDIUM SHOT - Karthik steps in.

CLOSE UP - HANDS. Small hands take the helmet from his.

MEDIUM CLOSE UP - Aadhya hugs the helmet, proud of her job.

WIDE SHOT - Sneha comes out of the kitchen with a glass of
water for him.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

5   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - DINING AREA - NIGHT

WIDE SHOT - Dinner. Warm practical light.

ALTERNATING MEDIUM SHOTS - Karthik and Sneha take turns
feeding Aadhya bites from their plates.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya, cheeks full, laughing. SOFT FOCUS on her
parents behind her.

WIDE SHOT - Laughter. A warm, ordinary, lovely evening.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

6   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - NIGHT

HIGH ANGLE, OVERHEAD - Lights low. Aadhya curls up against
Karthik, her head on his shoulder. Sneha lies on his other
side.

SLOW PUSH IN - They chat about the day, their voices growing
softer, until all three drift off. FOCUS SOFTENS as they
sleep.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

7   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - PRE-DAWN

INSERT - THE CLOCK: 5:45. Same framing as Scene 1.

                         KARTHIK (O.S.)
          Bye, Sneha.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya, half asleep, jolts awake. Anxious.

WIDE SHOT - She is out of bed in a flash.

                                                       CUT TO:

8   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

OVER THE SHOULDER (AADHYA) - REPEAT SETUP from Scene 2. Just
like yesterday: Karthik at the door, his back to her, helmet
on. FOCUS on the helmet.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya exhales. Calm returns, and a small smile
with it.

MEDIUM SHOT - She turns and goes back to bed.

                                                       CUT TO:

9   INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - DAY

INSERT - THE CLOCK: 3:30.

WIDE SHOT - REPEAT SETUP from Scene 3. Aadhya bursts in.

QUICK CUTS - Bag, lunch carrier and shoes all land on the
floor.

MEDIUM SHOT - She bounces onto the sofa.

                         SNEHA
          Aadhya. Get fresh first.

CLOSE UP - Ignored. As usual, Aadhya grabs the phone and
video calls Dad.

MEDIUM SHOT - Sneha watches with a fond smile and shakes her
head. This father and daughter love each other so much.

INSERT - PHONE SCREEN. The call connects.

                         AADHYA
          Hi, Dad!

EXTREME CLOSE UP - Aadhya. Her face drops into shock. SLOW
PUSH IN. She stares at the screen, frozen like a rock. FOCUS
LOCKS on her eyes; everything around her falls into blur.
Room sound drains away.

                         KARTHIK (ON PHONE)
          Hi, baby! How was school? How was
          your day?

CLOSE UP - LOCKED OFF. Aadhya doesn't answer. She doesn't
move. There is pure terror in her eyes.

MEDIUM SHOT - Sneha's smile fades. RACK FOCUS from Aadhya in
the foreground to Sneha behind her. Something is wrong.

TRACKING SHOT - She crosses over and takes the phone from
Aadhya's hand.

INSERT - PHONE SCREEN, SNEHA'S POV. Karthik stands in an open
raw material yard. His head is bare.

PUSH IN on the screen - Behind him, at a safe distance, a
massive machine stretches its arm over the yard. Huge rocks
tumble from it and crash to the ground. The machine ROARS.
Each rock lands with a booming THUD.

It is enough to frighten any child.

                         SNEHA
          She must have got scared looking at
          that big machine behind you. Don't
          worry. I'll explain it to her.

INSERT - PHONE SCREEN. Karthik nods and ends the call.

WIDE SHOT - Sneha in the foreground, phone in hand. DEEP
FOCUS: in the background, Aadhya is already walking away,
toward the bedroom.

                                                       CUT TO:

10  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - DAY

HIGH ANGLE, TOP SHOT - Aadhya lies on the bed, staring at the
ceiling, lost in thought.

WIDE SHOT - Sneha enters, framed in the doorway.

                         SNEHA
          Come on, Aadhya. You just came from
          school. Get fresh and eat something.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya. Nothing.

MEDIUM SHOT - Sneha sits beside her and rests a hand on her
arm.

INSERT - Then pulls it back. Hot.

CLOSE UP - She presses the back of her hand to Aadhya's neck,
then her forehead.

                         SNEHA
                    (playful)
          Oh, dear. You've got a fever. But
          don't worry. Mommy's here to take
          care of you. I'll make this fever
          go away.

INSERT - Sneha tucks a thermometer under Aadhya's arm.

WIDE SHOT - She hurries out and returns with a fresh towel
and a bowl of water.

EXTREME CLOSE UP - THE THERMOMETER. She checks it.

102°F.

CLOSE UP - Sneha's breath catches. She composes herself.

MEDIUM SHOT - She spoons syrup into Aadhya's mouth, and
begins a sponge bath.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya's eyes never leave the ceiling. FOCUS on
her eyes; Sneha's hand and the towel drift soft through the
foreground.

                         SNEHA
          Does it hurt anywhere?

CLOSE UP - Aadhya. No response. Not even a flicker.

                         SNEHA
          Are you feeling cold?

CLOSE UP - Sneha. Nothing. Fear creeps into her face. She
pushes it down and keeps sponging.

TIME CUT - WIDE SHOT. Time passes. The window light has
shifted. DING-DONG.

                         SNEHA
          That must be your dad.

MEDIUM SHOT - She sets the towel aside and leaves the room.

CLOSE UP - For the first time, Aadhya turns her head.

AADHYA'S POV - Her eyes follow her mother out the door until
she is gone.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya. Then her eyes stay fixed on the doorway.
FOCUS PULLS past her to the empty doorway, and holds.

                                                       CUT TO:

11  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

MEDIUM SHOT - Sneha opens the front door. Karthik walks in,
pulling off his helmet.

CLOSE UP - Karthik. Out of habit, his eyes search the room
for the little hands that always take it from him.

CLOSE UP - HANDS. REPEAT SETUP from Scene 4. No small hands
enter frame. He sets the helmet down himself.

                         KARTHIK
          Where's Aadhya?

                         SNEHA
          She's down with a fever. She's
          resting in the bedroom.

WIDE SHOT, HANDHELD - Karthik heads straight for the bedroom.

                                                       CUT TO:

12  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - CONTINUOUS

CLOSE UP - Aadhya's eyes are on the door.

AADHYA'S POV - The doorway. Karthik appears. FOCUS SNAPS to
him.

WIDE SHOT - She leaps off the bed and throws herself into his
arms.

CLOSE UP - Karthik. He holds her and feels the heat
radiating off her.

                         KARTHIK
          You're burning up, baby. Let's go to
          a doctor.

OVER THE SHOULDER (KARTHIK) - He looks to Sneha in the
doorway. She nods.

MEDIUM SHOT - Karthik scoops Aadhya up.

                                                       CUT TO:

13  EXT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

WIDE SHOT - The three of them climb into the car.

HIGH ANGLE - The car drives off toward the hospital. CAMERA
HOLDS as the taillights recede.

                                                       CUT TO:

14  INT. HOSPITAL - DOCTOR'S ROOM - NIGHT

WIDE SHOT - Aadhya sits on a stool beside the DOCTOR. Across
the desk, Karthik and Sneha sit side by side.

CLOSE UP - The Doctor listens to Aadhya's chest with a
stethoscope.

MEDIUM SHOT - Then he checks her eyes and tongue.

                         KARTHIK
          She had a very high fever when I
          got home. Now it's gone. It just
          came down, like a miracle.

MEDIUM SHOT - The Doctor turns to him.

                         DOCTOR
          You gave her medicine in the
          afternoon. That's why the
          temperature came down. If the fever
          comes back, give her these.

INSERT - He starts writing a prescription.

                         DOCTOR (CONT'D)
          And just to be sure, as a
          precaution, I'm writing some blood
          tests for her.

MEDIUM SHOT - He hands the prescription to Karthik.

                         KARTHIK
          Thank you, Doctor.

WIDE SHOT - Karthik and Sneha get up and leave. Aadhya skips
out behind them.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

15  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - DINING AREA - NIGHT

CLOSE UP - A plate of steaming idlis, fresh from Sneha's
kitchen. Steam curls through the frame.

WIDE SHOT - The family eats, chats and laughs.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya is herself again.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

16  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - NIGHT

HIGH ANGLE, OVERHEAD - REPEAT SETUP from Scene 6. All three
asleep together.

                                                       CUT TO:

17  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - MORNING

CLOSE UP - Aadhya asleep. Daylight already in the window.

                         KARTHIK (O.S.)
          Bye, Sneha!

WIDE SHOT - Aadhya jumps out of bed and runs.

                                                       CUT TO:

18  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

WIDE SHOT - Karthik is late, and it shows. He is still
inside, near the front door, flustered.

CLOSE UP - He snatches his bag from Sneha's hand.

MEDIUM SHOT - He rushes out. His head is bare. No helmet.

OVER THE SHOULDER (AADHYA) - REPEAT SETUP from Scene 2. He
doesn't notice Aadhya watching from the hall.

CLOSE UP - LOCKED OFF. Aadhya goes stiff. Frozen like a
rock. FOCUS LOCKS on her face; the hall around her goes soft.

MEDIUM SHOT - Sneha shuts the door, turns, and finds Aadhya
staring at it, terrified.

                         SNEHA
          Dad got late today, so he left in a
          hurry. Come, let's go back to sleep.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya. No response.

INSERT - Sneha takes her hand. Hot. The fever is back.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya just keeps staring at the door.

MEDIUM SHOT - Sneha leads her to the sofa, sits her down, and
gives her the medicine.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya's eyes never leave the door. FOCUS stays on
her eyes as Sneha moves, soft, through the foreground.

MEDIUM CLOSE UP - Scared now, Sneha grabs her phone and dials
Karthik.

                                                 SMASH CUT TO:

19  EXT. CITY ROAD - MORNING

WIDE SHOT - Morning traffic. Karthik rides his motorcycle,
bareheaded.

CLOSE UP - He glances at his watch. Late.

INSERT - He twists the throttle. The ENGINE REVS.

INSERT - His pocket. The phone BUZZES.

TRACKING SHOT, ALONGSIDE - Steering one-handed, he digs it
out and looks down at the screen.

LOW ANGLE, WIDE SHOT - A car ROARS past, cutting dangerously
close as it overtakes him at high speed.

CLOSE UP - Karthik flinches.

INSERT - He grabs the handlebar, phone still in his fist.

MEDIUM SHOT - He wrestles the bike steady.

CLOSE UP - Heart pounding. FOCUS on his eyes; the traffic
streaks soft behind him.

WIDE SHOT - He pulls over to the side of the road and
answers.

                         SNEHA (ON PHONE)
          Karthik, the fever's back. Aadhya's
          acting strange again, like
          yesterday. She's not saying
          anything, just staring in one
          direction. I'm scared. I don't know
          what to do.

                         KARTHIK
          Don't worry. I'm turning around.
          I'm coming.

WIDE SHOT - He hangs up, starts the bike, makes a U-turn, and
speeds back toward home.

                                                       CUT TO:

20  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - MORNING

WIDE SHOT - Karthik walks in. Aadhya sits on the sofa. He
sits down beside her.

                         KARTHIK
          What happened, baby? Why are you so
          quiet? Aren't you sleepy this early
          in the morning?

CLOSE UP - Aadhya says nothing.

MEDIUM SHOT - She lies down with her head in his lap and
falls asleep.

WIDE SHOT - Sneha comes over and sits beside Karthik.

                         SNEHA
          She's been like this since you left
          this morning. Silent. Not
          responding to me. I gave her the
          medicine the doctor prescribed, but
          the fever didn't come down. I don't
          understand why she's so quiet.

                         KARTHIK
          Don't worry. It's probably nothing.
          She's sleeping peacefully now. Let
          her rest. Her blood test reports
          come today anyway. Once she wakes
          up, we'll go see the doctor.

MEDIUM THREE SHOT - Still worried, Sneha rests her head on
Karthik's shoulder. CAMERA HOLDS.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

21  INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - LATER

MEDIUM THREE SHOT - SAME ANGLE. The whole family is asleep on
the sofa, just as they were.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya wakes in her dad's lap. She smiles.

MEDIUM SHOT - She shakes Sneha and Karthik awake.

CLOSE UP - Karthik presses his palm to her temple.

                         KARTHIK
          Wow, the fever's gone, dear! But
          let's visit the doctor once more,
          just to be sure. Your reports come
          today too. We'll show them to the
          doctor and get his opinion. Go get
          ready.

CLOSE UP - Aadhya gives him a lazy, deeply uninterested look.

                         KARTHIK (CONT'D)
          Let's bunk school today. After the
          doctor, we'll rest at home and go to
          the park in the evening.

CLOSE UP - A huge smile.

WIDE SHOT - Aadhya hops off his lap and heads for the
bathroom to get ready.

                                                       CUT TO:

22  INT. HOSPITAL - DOCTOR'S ROOM - DAY

WIDE SHOT - REPEAT SETUP from Scene 14. Aadhya sits beside
the Doctor. Karthik and Sneha sit across the desk.

INSERT - Aadhya's test reports lie between them. The Doctor
reads through them.

                         DOCTOR
          Everything looks fine.

                         SNEHA
          But Doctor, she gets fever all of a
          sudden. Her temperature shoots up,
          she goes stiff, she won't talk to
          anyone. I don't understand.

                         DOCTOR
          There's nothing to worry about here.
          The reports are all normal. It seems
          she saw or heard something that is
          scaring her badly. The fright is
          causing the fever. Gently ask her
          what frightened her.

MEDIUM SHOT - RACK FOCUS from the Doctor to Aadhya beside
him, quiet, eyes lowered.

                         KARTHIK AND SNEHA
          Thank you, Doctor.

WIDE SHOT - They leave. Aadhya follows behind them.

                                                       CUT TO:

23  EXT. CITY STREETS - DAY

MONTAGE - HANDHELD, QUICK CUTS. The family wanders the city
all day. Aadhya is thrilled to be skipping school.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

24  EXT. CHILDREN'S PARK - EVENING

WIDE SHOT - Golden hour. Aadhya plays on the slides and
swings, full of joy.

MEDIUM TWO SHOT - Karthik and Sneha watch her from a bench.
DEEP FOCUS: parents in the foreground, Aadhya swinging in the
background.

TRACKING SHOT - Aadhya spots them, skips over, squeezes in
between them, and rests her head on Karthik's shoulder.

MEDIUM THREE SHOT - The bench.

                         KARTHIK
          Baby, are you happy?

                         AADHYA
          Yes, Daddy.

                         KARTHIK
          Then why did you get fever, baby?
          Did you see or hear something that
          frightened you? Like the doctor
          said?

CLOSE UP - Aadhya nods.

                         KARTHIK (CONT'D)
          Will you tell me what frightened
          you?

                         AADHYA
                    (casually)
          It's you, Dad. Looking at you
          frightened me.

CLOSE UP - SLOW PUSH IN. Karthik is stunned and confused.

                         SNEHA
                    (trying to sound casual)
          How does your dad scare you, ra?

                         AADHYA
          I got frightened looking at Dad when
          he was leaving for office.

                         KARTHIK AND SNEHA
          We don't understand.

MEDIUM SHOT - Aadhya shifts a little closer to her mom.

                         AADHYA
          One day, when I was in the school
          van, going to school...

CLOSE UP - Aadhya's eyes drift into memory. FOCUS SOFTENS.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

25  EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET - MORNING (FLASHBACK)

NOTE: All flashback scenes graded cooler and desaturated.

WIDE SHOT - A school van rolls down the street, full of KIDS
in uniform, chatting and singing.

MEDIUM SHOT, INSIDE THE VAN - Aadhya is among them.

WIDE SHOT - The van stops at the gate of an independent house
with a small front lawn. The VAN DRIVER honks.

AADHYA'S POV, THROUGH THE VAN WINDOW - The lawn. On a chair
sits RAJU (late 30s), about her father's age.

SLOW TILT DOWN - Bandages wrap his head and fingers. Plaster
casts cover one arm and one leg. The arm rests in a sling. A
walking stick leans against his chair.

CLOSE UP - The driver honks again.

WIDE SHOT - The front door flies open. A 6-YEAR-OLD GIRL runs
out, a heavy school bag on her shoulders and a lunch carrier
in her right hand.

TRACKING SHOT - Halfway to the gate, she trips.

LOW ANGLE, GROUND LEVEL - She falls flat on her face. She
bursts into tears.

CLOSE UP - Raju strains to rise. He can't.

MEDIUM SHOT - With enormous effort and pain, he hauls himself
up on the walking stick, wobbling.

INSERT - His foot. One step toward his daughter.

WIDE SHOT - He loses his balance and crashes to the ground
beside her.

WIDE SHOT - RAJU'S WIFE (30s) rushes out of the house. She
picks up the girl first, then helps Raju to his feet.

CLOSE UP - He rises slowly, wincing.

MEDIUM SHOT - The girl wipes her tears and walks to the van.
Raju and his wife watch her climb aboard.

                         RAJU
                    (to his wife)
          I couldn't even help my own child
          when she fell and cried right in
          front of me. All because I wasn't
          careful. I wish I could turn back
          time.

CLOSE UP, FROM INSIDE THE VAN - RACK FOCUS from Aadhya's
reflection in the window glass to Raju's face beyond it.
Tears roll down his cheeks.

                                                       CUT TO:

26  INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - CONTINUOUS (FLASHBACK)

MEDIUM SHOT - The girl sits down beside Aadhya. The van pulls
away.

WIDE SHOT, HANDHELD - The other kids crowd around her,
curious and innocent.

                         KID 1
          What happened to your dad?

                         KID 2
          Why is he wrapped in so many
          bandages?

                         KID 3
          Why can't he walk?

                         GIRL
          My dad had an accident at the
          office where he works.

                         KID 1
          How?

CLOSE UP - SLOW PUSH IN. The girl begins to explain.

                                                  DISSOLVE TO:

27  INT. STEEL PLANT - YARD OFFICE - DAY (FLASHBACK WITHIN FLASHBACK)

NOTE: Harsh amber sodium vapor grade for this sequence.

WIDE SHOT - A dusty office lit by sodium vapor lamps. Cement
floor. Raju, healthy and whole, sits behind an iron table.

SLOW PAN - Papers, files, and in the corner, his safety
helmet. FOCUS on the helmet; Raju soft behind it.

MEDIUM SHOT - Against the opposite wall, five WORKERS sit on
a bench. One of them, WORKER 1, answers his phone.

                         WORKER 1
                    (into phone)
          Okay.
                    (to Raju)
          Sir, unloading of the material is
          almost done.

MEDIUM SHOT - Raju stands.

                         RAJU
          Let's go down and inspect it. See
          how they're stacking them.

TRACKING SHOT - He heads for the door.

INSERT - The helmet stays on the table. RACK FOCUS from the
helmet to Raju walking away in the background.

                         WORKER 2
          Sir, you forgot your safety shoes
          and helmet.

MEDIUM SHOT - Raju, at the door.

                         RAJU
          It's almost one o'clock. Lunch time.
          The inspection won't take more than
          five minutes. I'll only have to take
          it all off again for lunch. Why so
          much work? I'll finish quickly and
          come back.

WIDE SHOT - He walks out and heads down the stairs. CAMERA
HOLDS on the helmet left behind.

                                                       CUT TO:

28  INT. STEEL PLANT - BLOOM UNLOADING YARD - CONTINUOUS

EXTREME WIDE SHOT - A vast, dusty warehouse. Stacks of steel
blooms and pipes sit all across the floor.

WIDE SHOT - On one side, two massive overhead cranes lift
long steel blooms off an 18-wheeler and stack them.

MEDIUM SHOT - Beneath each crane, two RIGGERS in full safety
gear guide the operators.

LOW ANGLE - The blooms are so long that each one hangs from
two slings, one at each end.

TRACKING SHOT - Raju strolls down the middle of the
warehouse. No helmet, no goggles, no safety shoes.

QUICK INSERTS - His bare head. His unprotected eyes. His
ordinary shoes on the dusty floor.

LOW ANGLE, RAJU IN THE FOREGROUND - A crane carries a bloom
directly over his head.

EXTREME CLOSE UP - THE SLING. SNAP. One sling breaks.

SLOW MOTION - WIDE SHOT. That end of the bloom drops and the
whole bar swings like a pendulum, still hanging from the
other sling.

RAJU'S POV - Its free end hurtles straight at Raju.

CLOSE UP - The nearest RIGGER sees it.

MEDIUM SHOT, REAL TIME - He lunges and yanks Raju aside.

CLOSE UP - The bloom misses him by inches.

LOW ANGLE, FLOOR LEVEL - Raju hits the floor hard.

WIDE SHOT - The swinging bloom slams into a stack of pipes
and knocks it loose.

CLOSE UP - Raju, scrambling to get up, doesn't see it. FOCUS
on Raju in the foreground; the stack teeters, soft, behind
him.

EXTREME CLOSE UP - The second sling gives way.

WIDE SHOT - The bloom crashes to the floor with a deafening
