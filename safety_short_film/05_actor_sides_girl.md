# Actor Sides: GIRL (6)

**SAFETY** - An Original Short Film

## Emotional Arc

The Girl starts as an ordinary six-year-old rushing out to her school van, but her fall, and her father's helpless collapse beside her, expose the painful new reality at home. Surrounded by curious classmates, she bravely explains his accident, and her composure finally breaks into tears as she admits Daddy will never run and play with her like before.

> Scene numbers match the shooting script. In other characters' dialogue, "..." means their lines are trimmed to the final sentence (the cue).

```
25  EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET - MORNING (FLASHBACK)

A school van stops at the gate of an independent house with a
small front lawn. The VAN DRIVER honks.

On a chair on the lawn sits RAJU (late 30s), wrapped in
bandages, plaster casts on one arm and one leg. A walking
stick leans against his chair.

The driver honks again.

The front door flies open. A 6-YEAR-OLD GIRL runs out, a
heavy school bag on her shoulders and a lunch carrier in her
right hand. Halfway to the gate, she trips and falls flat on
her face. She bursts into tears.

Raju strains to rise. He hauls himself up on the walking
stick, takes one step toward his daughter, loses his balance,
and crashes to the ground beside her.

RAJU'S WIFE (30s) rushes out of the house. She picks up the
girl first, then helps Raju to his feet.

The girl wipes her tears and walks to the van. Raju and his
wife watch her climb aboard.

                         RAJU
                    (to his wife)
          ...I wish I could turn back time.


26  INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - CONTINUOUS (FLASHBACK)

The girl sits down beside AADHYA (8). The van pulls away.

The other kids crowd around her, curious and innocent.

                         KID 1
          What happened to your dad?

                         KID 2
          Why is he wrapped in so many
          bandages?

                         KID 3
          Why can't he walk?

                         GIRL
          My dad had an accident at the
          office where he works.

                         KID 1
          How?

The girl begins to explain.


29  INT. HOSPITAL - WARD - DAY (FLASHBACK WITHIN FLASHBACK)

Raju lies in a hospital bed, heavily bandaged. A COLLEAGUE
speaks quietly to Raju's Wife. The Girl stands beside her
mother.

                         COLLEAGUE
          ...If he had worn his PPE that day,
          the accident wouldn't have been this
          serious.


30  INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - DAY (FLASHBACK)

The girl finishes her story to the kids around her.

                         GIRL
          The doctor said it will take many
          months for Daddy to get better. And
          he said Daddy won't be able to walk
          properly like before. He won't be
          able to run and play with me like
          before.

Tears well up in her eyes. The kids fall quiet.
```
