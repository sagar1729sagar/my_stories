# Actor Sides: DOCTOR

**SAFETY** - An Original Short Film

## Emotional Arc

The Doctor is a calm, reassuring professional who first treats Aadhya's fever as routine and orders blood tests only as a precaution. On the second visit, finding every report normal, he moves from clinical certainty to gentle insight, recognising that fear rather than illness lies behind her fevers.

> Scene numbers match the shooting script. In other characters' dialogue, "..." means their lines are trimmed to the final sentence (the cue).

```
14  INT. HOSPITAL - DOCTOR'S ROOM - NIGHT

AADHYA (8) sits on a stool beside the DOCTOR. Across the
desk, KARTHIK (38) and SNEHA (30s) sit side by side.

The Doctor listens to Aadhya's chest with a stethoscope, then
checks her eyes and tongue.

                         KARTHIK
          ...It just came down, like a
          miracle.

The Doctor turns to him.

                         DOCTOR
          You gave her medicine in the
          afternoon. That's why the
          temperature came down. If the fever
          comes back, give her these.

He starts writing a prescription.

                         DOCTOR (CONT'D)
          And just to be sure, as a
          precaution, I'm writing some blood
          tests for her.

He hands the prescription to Karthik.

                         KARTHIK
          Thank you, Doctor.

Karthik and Sneha get up and leave. Aadhya skips out behind
them.


22  INT. HOSPITAL - DOCTOR'S ROOM - DAY

Aadhya sits beside the Doctor. Karthik and Sneha sit across
the desk. Aadhya's test reports lie between them. The Doctor
reads through them.

                         DOCTOR
          Everything looks fine.

                         SNEHA
          ...I don't understand.

                         DOCTOR
          There's nothing to worry about here.
          The reports are all normal. It seems
          she saw or heard something that is
          scaring her badly. The fright is
          causing the fever. Gently ask her
          what frightened her.

                         KARTHIK AND SNEHA
          Thank you, Doctor.

They leave. Aadhya follows behind them.
```
