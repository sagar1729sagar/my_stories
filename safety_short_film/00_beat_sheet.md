# SAFETY - Beat Sheet / Step Outline

**Source:** `safety_short_film/story.docx`

## Logline

An eight-year-old girl secretly checks every day that her steel-plant father is wearing his safety gear, and falls into silent, feverish terror each time she catches him without it. Only when her baffled parents finally get her to explain do they learn that a classmate's crippled father is the reason, and Karthik must face what his carelessness costs the people who love him.

## Beats

1. **Opening Image / Status Quo**
   - Before dawn, Karthik quietly dresses for the early shift while his daughter Aadhya drifts in and out of sleep. When she hears him say goodbye, she bolts to the hall, sees him strapping on his helmet, and her anxiety melts into a smile.

2. **Setup: The Daily Ritual**
   - Aadhya bursts home from school, ignores her mother Sneha entirely, and video-calls her father at the steel plant. He appears in a helmet and goggles, and the cheerful call leaves Sneha feeling invisible.

3. **Setup: The Happy Home**
   - Aadhya proudly takes Karthik's helmet when he comes home, and the family shares a warm dinner and falls asleep together. The picture of an ordinary, loving household is established.

4. **Repetition / Pattern Confirmed**
   - The next dawn, Aadhya again rushes out at her father's goodbye and is calmed only once she sees the helmet on his head. The ritual is clearly a habit, but its purpose stays hidden.

5. **Inciting Incident**
   - On the afternoon video call, Aadhya sees her father standing bareheaded in the yard while a huge machine drops rocks behind him. She freezes in terror, and Sneha takes the phone, assuming the noisy machine frightened her.

6. **Rising Action: The Mystery Fever**
   - Aadhya withdraws to her bed, staring at the ceiling, unresponsive, and Sneha discovers she is burning with a 102°F fever. Sneha treats her with forced cheer while hiding her growing fear.

7. **Rising Action: Father Returns**
   - When the doorbell rings, Aadhya finally moves, her eyes fixed on the doorway. Karthik notices no small hands reach for his helmet, finds his daughter feverish, and the family rushes to the hospital.

8. **False Resolution**
   - The doctor credits the earlier medicine for the fever breaking and orders blood tests as a precaution. Aadhya is herself again by dinner, and the crisis seems over.

9. **Complication: The Second Trigger**
   - Running late the next morning, Karthik grabs his bag and rushes out without his helmet, never noticing Aadhya watching. She goes rigid, the fever returns instantly, and a frightened Sneha calls him.

10. **Near Miss: The Danger Made Real**
    - Riding bareheaded and distracted by his ringing phone, Karthik is almost hit by a speeding car. Shaken, he pulls over, hears Sneha's panic, and turns back for home.

11. **Rising Tension: Helpless Parents**
    - Aadhya silently falls asleep in Karthik's lap while Sneha voices her confusion and fear. Karthik reassures her, but both are left waiting for answers they don't have.

12. **Midpoint: Medicine Has No Answer**
    - Aadhya wakes fever-free, and Karthik bribes her with a day off school to see the doctor again. The reports are normal, and the doctor reframes the problem: something has frightened her, and her parents must gently find out what.

13. **Breather / Reconnection**
    - The family spends a carefree day roaming the city and ends at the park, where Aadhya plays joyfully. The calm sets the stage for the confession.

14. **The Turn: "It's You"**
    - On the park bench, Karthik gently asks what scared her, and Aadhya casually reveals that he is the source of her fear. Her stunned parents press for meaning, and she begins her story.

15. **Backstory: The Broken Father**
    - From the school van, Aadhya once watched a bandaged, crippled man, Raju, try to help his fallen daughter and collapse beside her. His tearful regret over his own carelessness plants the seed of Aadhya's fear.

16. **Backstory Within Backstory: The Accident**
    - Raju waves off his helmet and safety shoes for a quick pre-lunch inspection of the bloom unloading yard. A crane sling snaps, a rigger saves him from the swinging bloom, but blinding dust and a collapsing pipe stack leave him crushed and unconscious.

17. **Backstory: The Lesson Lands**
    - A colleague tells Raju's wife the injuries are severe only because he skipped his PPE that one day. Back in the van, his daughter tearfully reveals he will never walk or play like before, and Aadhya stares out the window thinking of her own father.

18. **Backstory: The Vow Is Born**
    - Haunted all day, Aadhya asks Sneha what safety gear her father wears and memorises every item. She is relieved for now, but quietly becomes her father's self-appointed safety inspector.

19. **Climax: The Confession**
    - Back on the bench, Aadhya explains that the dawn checks and the daily calls are all to make sure her father is protected, and that seeing him bareheaded made her sick with fear. Asked about this morning, she says she saw him leave without his helmet, and Karthik is confronted by flashes of his own near miss.

20. **Character Transformation**
    - Realising the depth of his daughter's love and the weight of his choices, Karthik embraces her and vows never to ignore his safety gear again. Aadhya's fear finally dissolves into a joyful hug.

21. **Message Card 1**
    - On-screen text drives home that safety equipment is a responsibility, not just a rule, for the sake of the people who wait at home.

22. **Resolution / Final Image**
    - Walking to the car, Karthik offers to visit Raju's family, but Aadhya reveals they have left town because Raju had to quit his job. Karthik squeezes her hand tighter as they walk on, sobered by the lasting cost of a single careless day.

23. **Closing Message Card**
    - Final on-screen text: a company can replace a worker, but a family can never replace a loved one, so always make safety the highest priority.
