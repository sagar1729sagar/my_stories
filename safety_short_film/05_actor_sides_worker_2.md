# Actor Sides: WORKER 2

**SAFETY** - An Original Short Film

## Emotional Arc

Worker 2 is a conscientious, safety-minded junior who notices the helmet left on the table and respectfully speaks up to his superior. His polite reminder is brushed aside, making him the last voice of caution before the accident.

> Scene numbers match the shooting script. In other characters' dialogue, "..." means their lines are trimmed to the final sentence (the cue).

```
27  INT. STEEL PLANT - YARD OFFICE - DAY (FLASHBACK WITHIN FLASHBACK)

A dusty office lit by sodium vapor lamps. RAJU (late 30s)
sits behind an iron table. In the corner, his safety helmet.

Against the opposite wall, five WORKERS sit on a bench.
WORKER 1 answers his phone.

                         WORKER 1
                    (to Raju)
          Sir, unloading of the material is
          almost done.

Raju stands.

                         RAJU
          ...See how they're stacking them.

He heads for the door. The helmet stays on the table.

                         WORKER 2
          Sir, you forgot your safety shoes
          and helmet.

                         RAJU
          ...I'll finish quickly and come
          back.

He walks out and heads down the stairs.
```
