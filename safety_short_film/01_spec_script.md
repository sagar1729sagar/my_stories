                              SAFETY

                       An Original Short Film





INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - PRE-DAWN

Darkness. The house sleeps.

A cupboard door CREAKS open. KARTHIK (38) eases out his work
clothes, as quietly as he can. Morning shift today. He has
to be out before six.

In the bed, AADHYA (8) stirs. She isn't alarmed. She knows
this sound. Her eyes half open.

The wall clock opposite her reads 5:20.

She closes her eyes and sinks back into sleep.

Later. The front door CLICKS open. Aadhya's eyes open lazily
again.

The clock: 5:45.

                         KARTHIK (O.S.)
          Bye, Sneha.

Aadhya bolts upright. She scrambles out of bed and hurries
out of the room, anxious.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

Aadhya stops short. Through the open front door, Karthik
stands outside with his back to her, strapping on his
motorcycle helmet.

Her worried face softens into a small smile.

Karthik rides off. SNEHA (30s), Aadhya's mother, closes the
door and turns. She finds Aadhya standing there, smiling.

                         SNEHA
          You just missed Dad. He just left
          for office. Go back to sleep. I'll
          wake you up for school.

Aadhya nods and pads back toward the bedroom, still smiling.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - DAY

The wall clock reads 3:30. Sneha sits on the sofa, waiting.

The front door bursts open. Aadhya bounces in: school
uniform, bag on her back, lunch carrier swinging from one
hand.

Sneha gets up to take the load off her. Aadhya breezes right
past her. She dumps the bag and lunch carrier on the floor,
kicks off her shoes mid-stride, and hop-skips in her socks
to the sofa. She plops down and grabs Sneha's phone.

                         SNEHA
          Come on, Aadhya. You just came from
          outside. First take off those socks
          and dirty clothes and get fresh.

Aadhya doesn't hear a word. She opens WhatsApp and video
calls "DAD."

On the screen, Karthik appears in a safety helmet and
goggles. Behind him, the raw material yard of a steel plant.

                         AADHYA
          School's over for the day, Dad!

                         KARTHIK (ON PHONE)
          Nice. So how was school today?

                         AADHYA
          Good. It would've been amazing if
          there was no homework.

Karthik laughs.

                         KARTHIK (ON PHONE)
          Okay, dear. Finish your homework
          quickly, before I get home. Then
          we can go out and play.

                         AADHYA
          Okay, Dad!

She ends the call.

Sneha has watched the whole thing from across the room.

                         SNEHA
                    (to herself)
          Your mom is invisible to you.

She heads into the kitchen.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - EVENING

DING-DONG. Aadhya races to the door and pulls it open.
Karthik steps in. She takes the helmet from his hands, proud
of her job.

Sneha comes out of the kitchen with a glass of water for
him.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - DINING AREA - NIGHT

Dinner. Karthik and Sneha take turns feeding Aadhya bites
from their plates. Laughter. A warm, ordinary, lovely
evening.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - NIGHT

Lights low. Aadhya curls up against Karthik, her head on his
shoulder. Sneha lies on his other side. They chat about the
day, their voices growing softer, until all three drift off.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - PRE-DAWN

The clock: 5:45.

                         KARTHIK (O.S.)
          Bye, Sneha.

Aadhya, half asleep, jolts awake. Anxious. She is out of bed
in a flash.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

Just like yesterday: Karthik at the door, his back to her,
helmet on.

Aadhya exhales. Calm returns, and a small smile with it. She
turns and goes back to bed.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - DAY

3:30. Aadhya bursts in. Bag, lunch carrier and shoes all
land on the floor. She bounces onto the sofa.

                         SNEHA
          Aadhya. Get fresh first.

Ignored. As usual, Aadhya grabs the phone and video calls
Dad.

Sneha watches with a fond smile and shakes her head. This
father and daughter love each other so much.

The call connects.

                         AADHYA
          Hi, Dad!

Her face drops into shock. She stares at the screen, frozen
like a rock.

                         KARTHIK (ON PHONE)
          Hi, baby! How was school? How was
          your day?

Aadhya doesn't answer. She doesn't move. There is pure terror
in her eyes.

Sneha's smile fades. Something is wrong. She crosses over and
takes the phone from Aadhya's hand.

On the screen: Karthik stands in an open raw material yard.
His head is bare. Behind him, at a safe distance, a massive
machine stretches its arm over the yard. Huge rocks tumble
from it and crash to the ground. The machine ROARS. Each rock
lands with a booming THUD.

It is enough to frighten any child.

                         SNEHA
          She must have got scared looking at
          that big machine behind you. Don't
          worry. I'll explain it to her.

Karthik nods and ends the call.

Aadhya is already walking away, toward the bedroom.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - DAY

Aadhya lies on the bed, staring at the ceiling, lost in
thought.

Sneha enters.

                         SNEHA
          Come on, Aadhya. You just came from
          school. Get fresh and eat something.

Nothing. Sneha sits beside her and rests a hand on her arm,
then pulls it back. Hot. She presses the back of her hand to
Aadhya's neck, then her forehead.

                         SNEHA
                    (playful)
          Oh, dear. You've got a fever. But
          don't worry. Mommy's here to take
          care of you. I'll make this fever
          go away.

Sneha tucks a thermometer under Aadhya's arm. She hurries out
and returns with a fresh towel and a bowl of water. She
checks the thermometer.

102°F.

Sneha's breath catches. She composes herself, spoons syrup
into Aadhya's mouth, and begins a sponge bath.

Aadhya's eyes never leave the ceiling.

                         SNEHA
          Does it hurt anywhere?

No response. Not even a flicker.

                         SNEHA
          Are you feeling cold?

Nothing. Fear creeps into Sneha's face. She pushes it down
and keeps sponging.

Time passes. DING-DONG.

                         SNEHA
          That must be your dad.

She sets the towel aside and leaves the room.

For the first time, Aadhya turns her head. Her eyes follow
her mother out the door until she is gone. Then they stay
fixed on the doorway.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

Sneha opens the front door. Karthik walks in, pulling off his
helmet. Out of habit, his eyes search the room for the little
hands that always take it from him.

He sets the helmet down himself.

                         KARTHIK
          Where's Aadhya?

                         SNEHA
          She's down with a fever. She's
          resting in the bedroom.

Karthik heads straight for the bedroom.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - CONTINUOUS

Aadhya's eyes are on the door when Karthik appears. She
leaps off the bed and throws herself into his arms.

He holds her and feels the heat radiating off her.

                         KARTHIK
          You're burning up, baby. Let's go to
          a doctor.

He looks to Sneha in the doorway. She nods.

Karthik scoops Aadhya up.

EXT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

The three of them climb into the car and drive off toward the
hospital.

INT. HOSPITAL - DOCTOR'S ROOM - NIGHT

Aadhya sits on a stool beside the DOCTOR. Across the desk,
Karthik and Sneha sit side by side.

The Doctor listens to Aadhya's chest with a stethoscope, then
checks her eyes and tongue.

                         KARTHIK
          She had a very high fever when I
          got home. Now it's gone. It just
          came down, like a miracle.

The Doctor turns to him.

                         DOCTOR
          You gave her medicine in the
          afternoon. That's why the
          temperature came down. If the fever
          comes back, give her these.

He starts writing a prescription.

                         DOCTOR (CONT'D)
          And just to be sure, as a
          precaution, I'm writing some blood
          tests for her.

He hands the prescription to Karthik.

                         KARTHIK
          Thank you, Doctor.

Karthik and Sneha get up and leave. Aadhya skips out behind
them.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - DINING AREA - NIGHT

A plate of steaming idlis, fresh from Sneha's kitchen. The
family eats, chats and laughs. Aadhya is herself again.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - NIGHT

All three asleep together.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - BEDROOM - MORNING

                         KARTHIK (O.S.)
          Bye, Sneha!

Aadhya jumps out of bed and runs.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - CONTINUOUS

Karthik is late, and it shows. He is still inside, near the
front door, flustered. He snatches his bag from Sneha's hand
and rushes out. No helmet.

He doesn't notice Aadhya watching from the hall.

Aadhya goes stiff. Frozen like a rock.

Sneha shuts the door, turns, and finds Aadhya staring at it,
terrified.

                         SNEHA
          Dad got late today, so he left in a
          hurry. Come, let's go back to sleep.

No response. Sneha takes her hand. Hot. The fever is back.

Aadhya just keeps staring at the door.

Sneha leads her to the sofa, sits her down, and gives her the
medicine. Aadhya's eyes never leave the door.

Scared now, Sneha grabs her phone and dials Karthik.

EXT. CITY ROAD - MORNING

Morning traffic. Karthik rides his motorcycle, bareheaded. He
glances at his watch. Late. He twists the throttle.

His phone BUZZES in his pocket. Steering one-handed, he digs
it out and looks down at the screen.

A car ROARS past, cutting dangerously close as it overtakes
him at high speed.

Karthik flinches. He grabs the handlebar, phone still in his
fist, and wrestles the bike steady. Heart pounding, he pulls
over to the side of the road and answers.

                         SNEHA (ON PHONE)
          Karthik, the fever's back. Aadhya's
          acting strange again, like
          yesterday. She's not saying
          anything, just staring in one
          direction. I'm scared. I don't know
          what to do.

                         KARTHIK
          Don't worry. I'm turning around.
          I'm coming.

He hangs up, starts the bike, makes a U-turn, and speeds back
toward home.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - MORNING

Karthik walks in. Aadhya sits on the sofa. He sits down
beside her.

                         KARTHIK
          What happened, baby? Why are you so
          quiet? Aren't you sleepy this early
          in the morning?

Aadhya says nothing. She lies down with her head in his lap
and falls asleep.

Sneha comes over and sits beside Karthik.

                         SNEHA
          She's been like this since you left
          this morning. Silent. Not
          responding to me. I gave her the
          medicine the doctor prescribed, but
          the fever didn't come down. I don't
          understand why she's so quiet.

                         KARTHIK
          Don't worry. It's probably nothing.
          She's sleeping peacefully now. Let
          her rest. Her blood test reports
          come today anyway. Once she wakes
          up, we'll go see the doctor.

Still worried, Sneha rests her head on Karthik's shoulder.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - LATER

The whole family is asleep on the sofa, just as they were.

Aadhya wakes in her dad's lap. She smiles and shakes Sneha
and Karthik awake.

Karthik presses his palm to her temple.

                         KARTHIK
          Wow, the fever's gone, dear! But
          let's visit the doctor once more,
          just to be sure. Your reports come
          today too. We'll show them to the
          doctor and get his opinion. Go get
          ready.

Aadhya gives him a lazy, deeply uninterested look.

                         KARTHIK (CONT'D)
          Let's bunk school today. After the
          doctor, we'll rest at home and go to
          the park in the evening.

A huge smile. Aadhya hops off his lap and heads for the
bathroom to get ready.

INT. HOSPITAL - DOCTOR'S ROOM - DAY

Aadhya sits beside the Doctor. Karthik and Sneha sit across
the desk. Aadhya's test reports lie between them. The Doctor
reads through them.

                         DOCTOR
          Everything looks fine.

                         SNEHA
          But Doctor, she gets fever all of a
          sudden. Her temperature shoots up,
          she goes stiff, she won't talk to
          anyone. I don't understand.

                         DOCTOR
          There's nothing to worry about here.
          The reports are all normal. It seems
          she saw or heard something that is
          scaring her badly. The fright is
          causing the fever. Gently ask her
          what frightened her.

                         KARTHIK AND SNEHA
          Thank you, Doctor.

They leave. Aadhya follows behind them.

EXT. CITY STREETS - DAY

The family wanders the city all day. Aadhya is thrilled to be
skipping school.

EXT. CHILDREN'S PARK - EVENING

Aadhya plays on the slides and swings, full of joy. Karthik
and Sneha watch her from a bench.

Aadhya spots them, skips over, squeezes in between them, and
rests her head on Karthik's shoulder.

                         KARTHIK
          Baby, are you happy?

                         AADHYA
          Yes, Daddy.

                         KARTHIK
          Then why did you get fever, baby?
          Did you see or hear something that
          frightened you? Like the doctor
          said?

Aadhya nods.

                         KARTHIK (CONT'D)
          Will you tell me what frightened
          you?

                         AADHYA
                    (casually)
          It's you, Dad. Looking at you
          frightened me.

Karthik is stunned and confused.

                         SNEHA
                    (trying to sound casual)
          How does your dad scare you, ra?

                         AADHYA
          I got frightened looking at Dad when
          he was leaving for office.

                         KARTHIK AND SNEHA
          We don't understand.

Aadhya shifts a little closer to her mom.

                         AADHYA
          One day, when I was in the school
          van, going to school...

EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET - MORNING (FLASHBACK)

A school van rolls down the street, full of KIDS in uniform,
chatting and singing. Aadhya is among them.

The van stops at the gate of an independent house with a
small front lawn. The VAN DRIVER honks.

Through the window, Aadhya can see the lawn. On a chair sits
RAJU (late 30s), about her father's age. Bandages wrap his
head and fingers. Plaster casts cover one arm and one leg.
The arm rests in a sling. A walking stick leans against his
chair.

The driver honks again.

The front door flies open. A 6-YEAR-OLD GIRL runs out, a
heavy school bag on her shoulders and a lunch carrier in her
right hand. Halfway to the gate, she trips and falls flat on
her face. She bursts into tears.

Raju strains to rise. He can't. With enormous effort and
pain, he hauls himself up on the walking stick, wobbling. He
takes one step toward his daughter, loses his balance, and
crashes to the ground beside her.

RAJU'S WIFE (30s) rushes out of the house. She picks up the
girl first, then helps Raju to his feet. He rises slowly,
wincing.

The girl wipes her tears and walks to the van. Raju and his
wife watch her climb aboard.

                         RAJU
                    (to his wife)
          I couldn't even help my own child
          when she fell and cried right in
          front of me. All because I wasn't
          careful. I wish I could turn back
          time.

Tears roll down his cheeks.

INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - CONTINUOUS (FLASHBACK)

The girl sits down beside Aadhya. The van pulls away.

The other kids crowd around her, curious and innocent.

                         KID 1
          What happened to your dad?

                         KID 2
          Why is he wrapped in so many
          bandages?

                         KID 3
          Why can't he walk?

                         GIRL
          My dad had an accident at the
          office where he works.

                         KID 1
          How?

The girl begins to explain.

INT. STEEL PLANT - YARD OFFICE - DAY (FLASHBACK WITHIN FLASHBACK)

A dusty office lit by sodium vapor lamps. Cement floor. Raju,
healthy and whole, sits behind an iron table. Papers, files,
and in the corner, his safety helmet.

Against the opposite wall, five WORKERS sit on a bench. One
of them, WORKER 1, answers his phone.

                         WORKER 1
                    (into phone)
          Okay.
                    (to Raju)
          Sir, unloading of the material is
          almost done.

Raju stands.

                         RAJU
          Let's go down and inspect it. See
          how they're stacking them.

He heads for the door. The helmet stays on the table.

                         WORKER 2
          Sir, you forgot your safety shoes
          and helmet.

                         RAJU
          It's almost one o'clock. Lunch time.
          The inspection won't take more than
          five minutes. I'll only have to take
          it all off again for lunch. Why so
          much work? I'll finish quickly and
          come back.

He walks out and heads down the stairs.

INT. STEEL PLANT - BLOOM UNLOADING YARD - CONTINUOUS

A vast, dusty warehouse. Stacks of steel blooms and pipes sit
all across the floor. On one side, two massive overhead
cranes lift long steel blooms off an 18-wheeler and stack
them. Beneath each crane, two RIGGERS in full safety gear
guide the operators.

The blooms are so long that each one hangs from two slings,
one at each end.

Raju strolls down the middle of the warehouse. No helmet, no
goggles, no safety shoes.

A crane carries a bloom directly over his head.

SNAP. One sling breaks. That end of the bloom drops and the
whole bar swings like a pendulum, still hanging from the
other sling. Its free end hurtles straight at Raju.

The nearest RIGGER sees it. He lunges and yanks Raju aside.
The bloom misses him by inches. Raju hits the floor hard.

The swinging bloom slams into a stack of pipes and knocks it
loose. Raju, scrambling to get up, doesn't see it.

The second sling gives way. The bloom crashes to the floor
with a deafening THUD. A cloud of dust erupts.

Raju, just back on his feet, takes the dust full in the eyes.
Half blind, he stumbles straight toward the collapsing stack.
His foot lands on a rolling pipe. His legs go out from under
him and he falls.

Pipes roll off the top of the stack and rain down on him: his
legs, his arms, his head.

Raju lies still. Unconscious.

INT. HOSPITAL - WARD - DAY (FLASHBACK WITHIN FLASHBACK)

Raju lies in a hospital bed, heavily bandaged. A COLLEAGUE
speaks quietly to Raju's Wife. The Girl stands beside her
mother.

                         COLLEAGUE
          The damage is very serious. Sir is
          lucky to be alive. Every day, Sir
          wears all his safety PPE. The one
          time he ignored it, this happened.
          If he had worn his PPE that day, the
          accident wouldn't have been this
          serious.

INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - DAY (FLASHBACK)

The girl finishes her story to the kids around her.

                         GIRL
          The doctor said it will take many
          months for Daddy to get better. And
          he said Daddy won't be able to walk
          properly like before. He won't be
          able to run and play with me like
          before.

Tears well up in her eyes. The kids fall quiet.

Aadhya stares out the window, thinking about her own father.

INT. SCHOOL - CLASSROOM - DAY (FLASHBACK)

Aadhya sits at her desk, miles away. The words keep ringing
in her ears.

                         COLLEAGUE (V.O.)
          The one time he ignored it...

                         RAJU (V.O.)
          All because I wasn't careful.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - DAY (FLASHBACK)

Aadhya comes home, drops her bag, and runs straight to Sneha.

                         AADHYA
          Mom, does Dad use safety equipment
          at office?

                         SNEHA
          Of course he does. Why do you ask?
          Did you have a lesson about it?

                         AADHYA
          What kind of equipment does Dad use?

                         SNEHA
          Your dad wears a safety helmet to
          protect his head from falling
          things, industrial shoes to protect
          his feet, goggles to protect his
          eyes from dust, and ear plugs to
          protect his ears from the loud
          machines.

Aadhya takes in every word. Then, a small smile of relief.

EXT. CHILDREN'S PARK - EVENING (PRESENT)

Aadhya sits between her parents on the bench.

                         AADHYA
          I remembered everything you told me
          that day, Mom. Then I thought, what
          if Dad forgets to wear them one day?
          Or ignores it one day? He might have
          an accident like my friend's dad.
          He'll get hurt. He'll be in so much
          pain. He won't be able to play with
          me.
                    (beat)
          That's why every morning I run out
          to see if he's wearing his helmet
          when he leaves. That's why I video
          call him every day after school, to
          see if he's wearing his safety gear.
          That day when I called, Dad wasn't
          wearing his helmet. And behind him,
          rocks were falling from a machine. I
          got scared a rock would fly and hit
          Dad. I got so scared.

Sneha nods slowly. It all makes sense now. She smiles and
strokes Aadhya's hair.

                         SNEHA
          Oh. So that's why our lovely
          daughter got a fever.

                         KARTHIK
          Then why did you get fever this
          morning?

                         AADHYA
          Because I saw you leave for office
          without your helmet.

Karthik goes still.

INT. KARTHIK'S HOUSE - HALL - MORNING (FLASHBACK)

Karthik snatches his bag from Sneha and rushes out the door.
No helmet.

EXT. CITY ROAD - MORNING (FLASHBACK)

Karthik's eyes on his phone. The car roars past, inches away.

EXT. CHILDREN'S PARK - EVENING (PRESENT)

Karthik looks down at his daughter. Only now does he
understand how much she loves him. His face fills with
tenderness. He wraps his arms around her.

                         KARTHIK
          You don't have to worry about
          anything from now on. I promise you,
          I will never ignore my safety PPE
          again. I'll always wear it, wherever
          I go.

Aadhya looks up at him, then throws her arms around him in a
big hug.

Words appear:

     NEVER IGNORE SAFETY EQUIPMENT.

     ALWAYS WEAR YOUR SAFETY AND PROTECTIVE DEVICES, AND BE
     SAFE.

     IT IS NOT JUST A RULE.
     IT IS OUR RESPONSIBILITY.

     FOR OUR SAKE,
     AND FOR THE SAKE OF THE PEOPLE WHO LOVE US
     AND WAIT FOR US AT HOME.

EXT. CHILDREN'S PARK - PATHWAY - EVENING

The family walks down the path toward the park gate and their
car.

                         KARTHIK
          Do you remember where your friend
          from the van lives? We should visit
          them.

                         AADHYA
          She left the school, Dad. Her family
          moved to another city.

                         KARTHIK
          How do you know?

                         AADHYA
          One Sunday, when I was playing in
          the park, she came. She said they
          were leaving the next day. Her dad
          resigned. He couldn't go to office
          properly, and he wasn't comfortable
          working there with his injuries. So
          they're moving back to their
          hometown.

Karthik squeezes her hand a little tighter. They walk on
together.

Words appear:

     YOUR COMPANY HAS MANY WORKERS LIKE YOU.
     BUT YOUR FAMILY HAS ONLY YOU.

     YOU CAN BE REPLACED EASILY AT YOUR WORKPLACE.
     BUT NO ONE CAN REPLACE YOU IN YOUR FAMILY.

     ALWAYS BE SAFE. GIVE SAFETY THE HIGHEST PRIORITY IN LIFE.

                                                       THE END
