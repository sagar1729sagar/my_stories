## Scheduling Notes

- **Karthik's House (21 scenes, 7 4/8 pages):** this is the biggest block. Plan 2 to 3 days. Shoot the pre-dawn and night scenes together on blacked-out windows, then the day and evening scenes. Aadhya's limited hours as a minor set the pace here.
- **Repeat setups:** shoot Scene 1 with Scene 7, Scene 2 with Scenes 8 and 18, and Scene 3 with Scene 9. Scene 11 matches Scene 4's helmet handoff, and Scene 16 matches Scene 6. Doing these back to back means the lens, height and framing only need to be locked once.
- **Steel plant (Sc. 27, 28 and the Sc. 3/9 phone footage):** this needs a plant permit and a plant safety officer. Shoot the video-call footage of Karthik **before** Scenes 3 and 9 so it can play live on the phone. Scene 28 is the stunt and VFX day; a stunt coordinator is required, and no real load may pass over the actor.
- **City Road (Sc. 19, 35):** needs a road closure or controlled traffic permit, a stunt rider and driver, and a tracking vehicle. Scene 35 reuses Scene 19's footage.
- **Children's Park (Sc. 24, 33, 36, 37):** shoot only at golden hour, which may take two evenings to protect the light continuity.
- **Raju looks:** schedule the healthy Raju scenes (27, 28) and the injured Raju scenes (25, 29) on separate days, or allow time to apply the injury makeup.
- **Minors:** Aadhya, the Girl and Kids 1-3 each need a guardian on set. The Girl's fall in Scene 25 needs a padded surface and a child safety supervisor.
- **Wardrobe continuity:** track the school uniforms (Sc. 3, 9, 25, 26, 30, 31, 32), Karthik's work clothes, and the helmet on/off status in every scene (on in 2, 4, 8 and 11; missing in 18, 19, 34 and 35).
