# Actor Sides: COLLEAGUE

**SAFETY** - An Original Short Film

## Emotional Arc

The Colleague stands at the hospital bedside, shaken by what has happened to a senior he respects, and tries to comfort the family by explaining it quietly. Beneath his gentle delivery is sorrow and frustration that a man who always wore his PPE was undone by the one time he didn't, a truth that lingers in a child's mind long afterwards.

> Scene numbers match the shooting script. In other characters' dialogue, "..." means their lines are trimmed to the final sentence (the cue).

```
29  INT. HOSPITAL - WARD - DAY (FLASHBACK WITHIN FLASHBACK)

RAJU (late 30s) lies in a hospital bed, heavily bandaged. A
COLLEAGUE speaks quietly to RAJU'S WIFE (30s). The GIRL (6)
stands beside her mother.

                         COLLEAGUE
          The damage is very serious. Sir is
          lucky to be alive. Every day, Sir
          wears all his safety PPE. The one
          time he ignored it, this happened.
          If he had worn his PPE that day, the
          accident wouldn't have been this
          serious.


31  INT. SCHOOL - CLASSROOM - DAY (FLASHBACK)

AADHYA (8) sits at her desk, miles away. The words keep
ringing in her ears.

                         COLLEAGUE (V.O.)
          The one time he ignored it...

                         RAJU (V.O.)
          All because I wasn't careful.
```
