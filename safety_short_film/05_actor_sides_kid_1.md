# Actor Sides: KID 1

**SAFETY** - An Original Short Film

## Emotional Arc

Kid 1 is a chatty, curious schoolmate whose innocent questions about the bandaged father open up the Girl's story, first asking what happened and then pressing for how. That eager curiosity turns into stunned silence as the Girl's tears reveal how serious the accident really was.

> Minor. Scene numbers match the shooting script. In other characters' dialogue, "..." means their lines are trimmed to the final sentence (the cue).

```
25  EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET - MORNING (FLASHBACK)

A school van rolls down the street, full of KIDS in uniform,
chatting and singing. AADHYA (8) is among them.

The van stops at the gate of an independent house. On a chair
on the lawn sits RAJU (late 30s), wrapped in bandages,
plaster casts on one arm and one leg.

A 6-YEAR-OLD GIRL runs out, trips and falls flat on her face.
Raju tries to reach her and crashes to the ground beside her.
RAJU'S WIFE (30s) rushes out and helps them both up.

The girl wipes her tears and walks to the van.


26  INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - CONTINUOUS (FLASHBACK)

The girl sits down beside Aadhya. The van pulls away.

The other kids crowd around her, curious and innocent.

                         KID 1
          What happened to your dad?

                         KID 2
          Why is he wrapped in so many
          bandages?

                         KID 3
          Why can't he walk?

                         GIRL
          My dad had an accident at the
          office where he works.

                         KID 1
          How?

The girl begins to explain.


30  INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - DAY (FLASHBACK)

The girl finishes her story to the kids around her.

                         GIRL
          ...He won't be able to run and play
          with me like before.

Tears well up in her eyes. The kids fall quiet.
```
