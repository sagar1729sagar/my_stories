# Actor Sides: VAN DRIVER

**SAFETY** - An Original Short Film

## Emotional Arc

The Van Driver is a routine, slightly impatient presence on the school run, honking to hurry the household along. He witnesses the family's painful struggle at the gate and quietly drives on, a silent observer of the aftermath.

> Non-speaking role. Scene numbers match the shooting script. In other characters' dialogue, "..." means their lines are trimmed to the final sentence.

```
25  EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET - MORNING (FLASHBACK)

A school van rolls down the street, full of KIDS in uniform,
chatting and singing.

The van stops at the gate of an independent house with a
small front lawn. The VAN DRIVER honks.

On a chair on the lawn sits RAJU (late 30s), wrapped in
bandages, plaster casts on one arm and one leg.

The driver honks again.

The front door flies open. A 6-YEAR-OLD GIRL runs out.
Halfway to the gate, she trips and falls flat on her face.
She bursts into tears.

Raju hauls himself up on his walking stick, loses his
balance, and crashes to the ground beside her. RAJU'S WIFE
(30s) rushes out and helps them both up.

The girl wipes her tears and walks to the van. Raju and his
wife watch her climb aboard.


26  INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - CONTINUOUS (FLASHBACK)

The girl sits down beside AADHYA (8). The van pulls away.

The other kids crowd around her, curious and innocent.

                         KID 1
          How?

The girl begins to explain.


30  INT. SCHOOL VAN - MOVING - DAY (FLASHBACK)

The girl finishes her story to the kids around her.

                         GIRL
          ...He won't be able to run and play
          with me like before.

Tears well up in her eyes. The kids fall quiet.
```
